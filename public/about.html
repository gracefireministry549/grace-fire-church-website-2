Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry exists to help people find hope, grow in faith and walk in His grace.

WHO WE ARE

Built on worship, the Word and prayer.

We are a Christian ministry committed to worship, prayer, the Word of God, deliverance, healing and helping people grow in faith. Through nine years of God's faithfulness, our church has become a warm family where newcomers are welcomed as friends and friends are discipled as sons and daughters of the Kingdom.

We warmly welcome you to fellowship with us and become part of our church family. Come as you are — there is a place for you here.
